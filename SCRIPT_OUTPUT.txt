PART I.  THE DIRECT READ FAILS, DIAGNOSTICALLY
==============================================================================
  The consensus test (McCowan/Hanser/Doyle 1999) has TWO axes:
    Zipf slope - how constraint distributes over symbol frequency
    depth      - how many symbols back still reduce uncertainty
  Both are required. Neither alone indicates language.

  bulk burst parameters                  Zipf   depth   ratio
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  FRB 20201124A                        -0.141       1   -0.00
  FRB 121102                           -0.356      20    0.47
  written English                       -1.00       9     low
  Vela pulsar                           -0.30       0

  Neither source is language on the direct read. Equiprobable symbols
  are what a channel optimised for capacity produces; a constraint that
  does not weaken with distance is what a frame header does.

  Symbol uniformity, outer layer: 9 types, mean 11.11%, CV = 0.1022

PART V.  ZIPF BY MAXIMUM LIKELIHOOD, WITH GOODNESS OF FIT
==============================================================================
  OLS log-log on rank-frequency cannot say whether the data obey a
  power law at all (Clauset 2009), and rank-frequency is the wrong
  representation to fit (Corral 2020). Fit the SIZE distribution.

  Implemented as Clauset prescribes: full xmin scan, SEMIPARAMETRIC
  bootstrap, every synthetic re-fitted with its own xmin and alpha,
  and a Vuong likelihood-ratio test against the lognormal.

  corpus                   alpha  xmin    KS D   GOF p    n   LR z   LR p   verdict
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  stream 1                 2.498    12   0.090   0.855   11   1.26  0.209   PLAUSIBLE power law; lognormal not distinguished
  stream 2                 2.386    23   0.095   0.852   11   1.17  0.242   PLAUSIBLE power law; lognormal not distinguished
  written English           2.00

  The fitted tails are small (n ~ 20), inside the regime Clauset et
  al. caution about: a high GOF p at this n means NOT REJECTED, not
  proven. For calibration of how demanding strict power-law testing
  is: under Moreno-Sanchez et al.'s (2016) full-domain protocols only
  ~40% of real English books pass in CCDF form, ~15% in PMF form.
  Their protocol differs from this tail test; the numbers calibrate
  strictness, they are not a same-test comparison.

PART VI.  THE MEMORYLESS-MARGINAL CONTROL (Sproat's decisive test)
==============================================================================
  A memoryless sequence with a skewed marginal can land inside the
  language range on ABSOLUTE conditional entropy. But for an i.i.d.
  sequence H(x_t|x_{t-1}) = H(x_t) exactly, so every conditional-entropy
  REDUCTION is zero by construction. The control that defeats an
  absolute-entropy claim cannot touch a reduction claim.

    k  real h_k   iid h_k     gap       z     beat
  --------------------------------------------------
    0     1.839     1.839  -0.000    -0.0    98/200
    1     1.027     1.834   0.807    48.4     0/200
    2     0.985     1.819   0.834    54.2     0/200
    3     0.946     1.759   0.813    50.4     0/200
  Matched at k=0 by construction; separated by ~0.8 bits at every
  order above. NOTE: orders 4 and 5 are NOT shown, because there the
  CONTROL's own plug-in estimator collapses and the gap shrinks for a
  reason that has nothing to do with the signal. Never difference
  against a collapsing control; compare levels.

  READ THIS TABLE IN TWO PARTS.
  The redundancy rows and the brevity row are SURROGATE-VALIDITY
  CHECKS, not null results. Order-1 redundancy is a deterministic
  function of the marginal and transition matrix, and brevity is a
  run-length statistic; the surrogate is built from exactly those, so
  agreement is arithmetic. A surrogate failing there would be broken.
  The evidence is the predictability gain, in bits:
  G1 = h1 - h2 = I(x_t; x_{t-2} | x_{t-1})  (De Gregorio, Sanchez &
  Toral 2026). The one quantity a persistence-matched surrogate cannot
  reproduce, and the direct answer to 'is this just persistence?'

PART IX.  THE MEASUREMENT IS FAR INSIDE ITS LIMITS
==============================================================================
  Max reliable block order r_max = floor(ln N / ln K) = 5   (N=1863, K=4)
  Every block-conditional-entropy claim above is at order <= 3.
  Plug-in MI bias floor (K-1)^2/2N = 0.00242 nats = 0.0035 bits
  against a measured lag-1 MI of 0.813 bits: a 233-fold margin.
  The i.i.d. control's own h_k drifting from k=1 to k=3 measures that
  bias directly and it is an order of magnitude below the real gap.

  Two measurement objects, kept apart deliberately:
    conditional entropy and depth -> the K=4 burst symbol stream
    Zipf, growth, brevity         -> the word types (distributional
                                     laws, not context-order bounded)
  Running conditional entropy on the 61-type word corpus would put
  r_max at 1 and the bias floor above the measurement.
